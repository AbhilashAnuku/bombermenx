How the multiplayer works

The experience this prototype walks through
🔗
Everyone joins one match
Each player's app connects to the same shared game, so all players share one arena.
🪑
Lobby & bots
A match needs 2–4 players. Open seats are filled by non-random bots, and a countdown starts the match.
🔄
Everyone stays in sync
A single shared game keeps every player's screen showing the same up-to-date world.
⚖️
Fair by design
Moves, bombs and scores are decided centrally, so no player can cheat or fall out of sync.
⏱️
Real-time
The arena updates many times a second — bombs, blasts and pick-ups happen live for everyone.
🏁
Result & ranking
When one player remains, the match ends and the final standings are shown to all players.
